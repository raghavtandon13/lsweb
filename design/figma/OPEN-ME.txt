Designer rules in this file
---------------------------
  Nav: Loans → Credit → Partner with us → Help last
  Header CTAs: Check credit score (gold) · Login · Apply now
  No-offer is calm navy, never red. Always a next step
    (gold loan, card against FD, credit health).
  Red is for form errors only.
  Empty lists get an Apply now CTA.
  Unauthenticated dashboard never shows empty chrome — it goes to login.
  Help tabs are visible pills, not faint underlines.
  We are not the lender — footer disclaimer stays on legal pages.
